## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-fashion, diagnostic intelligence environment tailored for discerning online apparel shoppers experiencing decision fatigue or fit uncertainty. The visual language merges haute couture editorial minimalism with hyper-refined dark mode glassmorphism and predictive AI diagnostics.

### Aesthetic Principles
- **Editorial Precision**: Generous negative space, razor-thin structural dividers, and deliberate micro-typography evoke high-end runway catalogs and luxury digital flagships.
- **Translucent Optics**: Multi-layered dark glass surfaces blur underlying fashion imagery, delivering spatial depth without visual clutter.
- **Diagnostic Confidence**: Decisive chromatic indicators (Crimson Magenta, Amber Glow, Precision Emerald) cut through atmospheric dark surfaces to provide instant semantic feedback on fit, styling compatibility, and wardrobe match rate.
- **Tactile Fluidity**: Softly luminescent interactive feedback, rounded pill elements, and polished borders create a touch-first, frictionless experience on mobile viewports.

## Colors

The color architecture is built strictly for dark mode, relying on low-reflectance, ultra-deep backgrounds offset by controlled luminescence and high-contrast typographic hierarchy.

### Base Surfaces
- **Canvas Base**: `#0a0a0f` — Absolute dark ground with a subtle blue-violet undertone.
- **Secondary Surface**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` — Subtle demarcation for segmented content, recessed controls, and underlays.
- **Glass Panel**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` — Translucent interactive substrate with backdrop-filter blur.
- **Elevated Glass**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` — Popovers, active match cards, and elevated recommendation overlays.

### Accents & Semantics
- **Primary Brand Accent (Myntra Pink)**: `#ff3f6c` — Used for high-intent conversion actions, key predictive match highlights, and primary system emphasis.
- **Secondary Accent (Amber Glow)**: `#f5a623` — Denotes conditional match scores, style cautions, price/hesitation nudges, and transitional states.
- **Success / High Compatibility (Precision Emerald)**: `#27ae60` — 90%+ wardrobe fit score, verified sizing, and low return probability indicators.
- **Border / Edge Stroke**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` — Sub-pixel perimeter lines defining structural glass panels.

### Text & Iconography
- **Text Primary**: `#f0f0f0` — High-clarity editorial text, headline copy, and definitive match labels.
- **Text Secondary**: `#a0a0a0` — Supporting body copy, metadata, and specification labels.
- **Text Muted**: `#666666` — Inactive controls, disclaimers, and contextual captions.

## Typography

Inter serves as the sole typographic foundation across all weights and viewports. The typographic system relies on tight tracking in display scales and calibrated optical leading in body sizes to reflect technical, computational fashion curation.

### Typographic Treatment
- **Display & Headlines**: Tightly tracked (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) with heavy font weight (`600`–`700`). Titles remain compact to facilitate dense data presentation without overwhelming the eye.
- **Body Text**: Rendered primarily in `#f0f0f0` for core text and `#a0a0a0` for descriptive copy. Normal tracking ensures legibility against low-luminance backdrops.
- **Labels & Micro-data**: Rendered with expanded letter-spacing (`0.04em`–`0.08em`) and uppercase transformations when marking match categories, fit metrics, or AI certainty indices.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout system is mobile-first, engineered around responsive touch zones and vertical thumb-reach optimization. 

### Grid Architecture
- **Mobile (Base to 640px)**: 4-column fluid grid, `16px` margins, `12px` gutters. Primary actions and AI resolution summaries anchor to the bottom 25% of the viewport.
- **Tablet (641px to 1024px)**: 8-column fluid grid, `24px` margins, `16px` gutters. Screen splits horizontally into dual focus areas: wardrobe contextual gallery on the left, match diagnostics panel on the right.
- **Desktop (1025px+)**: 12-column grid capped at a maximum width of `1280px`, centered. Side-by-side comparison arrays with a persistent glass control hub.

### Spacing Rhythm
Built upon an atomic `4px` baseline (`0.25rem`), spacing steps scale logarithmically for component interiors (`4px`, `8px`, `12px`, `16px`) and structural block separation (`24px`, `32px`, `48px`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy operates via physical illumination and optical transmission rather than opaque cast shadows. Surfaces float on progressive translucent steps over `#0a0a0f`.

### Surface Layering Hierarchy
1. **Base Floor (L0)**: `#0a0a0f`, flat, solid.
2. **Sub-surface (L1)**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` with a `1px` border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`. Used for passive collection containers, table rows, and unfocused states.
3. **Interactive Glass (L2)**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` overlaid with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a directional top highlight border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`. Applied to decision cards, hesitation resolution modules, and match sliders.
4. **Active Elevation (L3)**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` overlaid with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)` and a soft ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`. Applied to bottom sheets, active modals, and high-priority AI verdict banners.

### Light & Glow Dynamics
- **Accent Glow**: High-intent modules utilize an edge-glow driven by the dominant semantic color:
  - Match High: `0 0 20px -4px rgba(39, 174, 96, 0.25)`
  - Match Moderate / Alert: `0 0 20px -4px rgba(245, 166, 35, 0.25)`
  - Primary Action / Instant Fix: `0 0 24px -2px rgba(255, 63, 108, 0.35)`

## Shapes

The design system incorporates full pill-shaped curves (`roundedness: 3`) for interactive chips, primary CTA buttons, and status tokens, contrasting against soft-cornered glass viewport panels (`16px` to `24px`).

### Component Geometry
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Metric Badges)**: Full pill radius (`9999px`) to reinforce tactile touch targets and mobile ergonomics.
- **Cards & Analysis Containers**: `16px` to `20px` radius on mobile devices, scaling to `24px` on desktop containers.
- **Bottom Sheets & Floating Modals**: `24px` top radius on mobile views, nested securely within the safe screen bounds.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Confirm & Add", "Instant Match")**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), background `#ff3f6c`, text `#ffffff`, high-contrast semibold label. Interactive hover/press applies a dynamic outer halo `0 0 20px rgba(255, 63, 108, 0.4)`.
- **Secondary CTA ("Compare Alternative")**: Translucent glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`), `1px` border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#f0f0f0`. Active state brightens background to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Ghost/Destructive**: Transparent surface with `#a0a0a0` text transitioning to `#ff3f6c` on intentional triggers.

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Filter & Category Chips**: Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`, `1px` border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, font `label-md`. 
- **Selected State**: Border switches to `#ff3f6c`, fill transitions to `rgba(255, 63, 108, 0.12)`, text illuminates to `#ffffff`.
- **Confidence Metric Badges**: Compact pill containing an inline SVG dot indicator (`#27ae60` for 95%+, `#f5a623` for 70–94%) with high-contrast numerical tracking.

### Cards & Decision Panels
- **Match Diagnosis Card**: Layered glass base (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`), `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, `1px` border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Features an integrated progress ring or confidence meter at the top-right corner.
- **Wardrobe Synergy Card**: Compact split layout containing the prospective garment thumbnail on the left and a micro-carousel of existing owned items on the right, linked by dynamic compatibility badges.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Query & Size Input**: Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`, rounded `9999px` for search bars and `12px` for attribute dropdowns. Text primary `#f0f0f0`, placeholder `#666666`. Focus state initiates a continuous border highlight in `#ff3f6c`.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- **Toggle Switch**: Track uses `#0a0a0f` with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` border. Thumb is a luminous circle sliding into `#ff3f6c` active fill with an ambient pink glow.
- **Checkboxes**: Pill-rounded squircle (`6px`), inactive stroke `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`, active state filled with `#ff3f6c` displaying a crisp white micro-checkmark.

### Specialized Component: Hesitation Diagnostic Meter
- A circular radial or horizontal segmented gauge displaying match confidence percentages (e.g., "94% Style Compatibility", "Fit Alert: Runs Narrow"). Uses `#27ae60` for safe fit, `#f5a623` for subjective fit warnings, and `#ff3f6c` for direct conflicts with previous shopper purchase returns.
